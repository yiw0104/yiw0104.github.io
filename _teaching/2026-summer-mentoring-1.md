I served as a Postdoctoral Mentor for the [2026 Center for the Environment Summer Undergraduate Research Program](https://environment.washu.edu/2026-summer-undergraduate-researchers/), where I supervised undergraduate student Eliana Oden on a spatial mapping project examining subsidized housing and extreme heat exposure. I trained Eliana in secondary data collection and cleaning, spatial data linkage, and academic writing, resulting in a [research poster](https://yiw0104.github.io/files/CfE_Poster_Eliana_Oden.pdf) and a manuscript in progress.
